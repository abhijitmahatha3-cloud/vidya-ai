Topic: Why do astronauts float in orbit?

1. Gravity is still there! At the ISS altitude (~400 km), Earth's gravity is still ~90% as strong as on the ground.
2. Constant free-fall: The station moves sideways at ~28,000 km/h—so fast that as gravity pulls it down, Earth curves away beneath it.
3. Result: Both the astronaut and the station fall together at the exact same rate, creating weightlessness!
